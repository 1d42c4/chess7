## How to use the status column

Change `Ready to study` to something useful as you work:

- `Studying`
- `First pass complete`
- `Reviewing`
- `Personally reviewed`
- `Needs correction`

The point is not to create administrative work. The point is to distinguish generated material from material that has been seriously studied and checked.
