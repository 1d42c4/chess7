Scandinavian Defense — Black Field Manual

140 short lessons for Black. The practical backbone is 1.e4 d5 2.exd5 Qxd5 3.Nc3 Qa5. The goal is not to memorize a queen dance. It is to understand why Black challenges e4 immediately, how to lose as few tempi as possible, where the light bishop belongs, and when the freeing break ...c5 or ...e5 should transform the position.

140 lessons14 modules28-scenario combat reviewQa5 main repertoire2...Nf6 + gambit literacy

Main repertoire. 1.e4 d5 2.exd5 Qxd5 3.Nc3 Qa5, then rapid ...Nf6, an active light bishop on f5/g4 when appropriate, ...c6/e6 as needed, short castling, and a timely ...c5 or ...e5 break. Qd6 and Qd8 are taught as alternatives rather than mixed randomly into the main repertoire.
Optional weapons. The Portuguese and Icelandic-Palme gambits are included because they are fun and instructive, but they are clearly separated from the main sound Qa5 repertoire. Material compensation must be concrete.
