Sources & Scope

Scope. This is a practical Black repertoire plus theory-literacy course. The main repertoire is the classical Mieses–Kotrč family with 2...Qxd5 and 3...Qa5. Qd6/Qd8, 2...Nf6, Portuguese and Icelandic structures are taught so they are not blind spots.
