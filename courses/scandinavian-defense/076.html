White c4 Expansion

08 — Portuguese Variation: 2...Nf6 3.d4 Bg4

Mission: reach a healthy Scandinavian structure, minimize useless queen tempi, and know the exact central break that frees your position.

TriggerYou reach you choose the sharp Portuguese setup with 2...Nf6 3.d4 Bg4 and the immediate question is: white c4 expansion.
Field orderAttack d5 with ...e6 or c6 structures and target White's broad center.
WhyThe Portuguese develops with tempo against White's center and can create dangerous initiative, but White has concrete ways to challenge the bishop and Black must value development over pawn recovery. Attack d5 with ...e6 or c6 structures and target White's broad center.
Enemy intentWhite's best practical reply is usually to develop with tempo, preserve central space, or force your queen/pieces to lose time. Identify which of those White is trying before you move.
Abort / warningDo not let White keep both extra pawn and space uncontested.
30-second drill
Cover the answer. State: (1) where the queen belongs, (2) whether your light bishop should go to f5 or g4, (3) White's central pawn break, and (4) whether your freeing break is ...c5 or ...e5.
