King's Gambit — 32-Scenario Combat Review

For each position say: material count, Black's strongest check/capture/threat, your fastest developing move, your central break, and whether the attack still justifies the material.

Scenario 1: Why f4 Deflects e5

Situation: You reach you have played 1.e4 e5 2.f4 and must understand the transaction before choosing tactics and the current problem is: why f4 deflects e5.

Task: count material, name Black's strongest forcing move, state your central break and decide whether compensation is tactical or positional.

Reveal field answer

Order: If Black captures, use the removal of the e5 pawn to build d4/e4 central control and open lines.

Risk: The center can become White's compensation only if it is occupied quickly.

Warning: Do not spend time trying to recover f4 before developing.
