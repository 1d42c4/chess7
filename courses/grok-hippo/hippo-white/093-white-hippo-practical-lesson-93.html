Hippo from White · Lesson 093 · Direct training

This is a focused practical lesson for the White Hippo system. The core remains unchanged: reach a solid double-fianchetto shape (pawns on the third rank, bishops on b2/g2, knights ideally on d2/e2), then use the extra tempo to time the key breaks (c4 or f4) better than Black can organise a counter.

Apply the same decision process you use as Black, only with the advantage of the first move. Complete the shape, evaluate Black’s centre, choose the cleanest break, and improve the worst piece in between.

Extra tempo + solid structure + timed break = practical winning chances. Stay disciplined.
