Move Orders for White: 1.g3 vs 1.b3

Hippo from White · Lesson 007 · Direct training

1.g3 is more common and flexible. It can transpose into Catalan, King’s Indian Attack, or pure Hippo.

1.b3 (Nimzo-Larsen) is more forcing on the queenside and can lead to very original positions. Both are fully viable for a White Hippo player.

Choose based on whether you prefer kingside or queenside emphasis in the early moves.

Either starting move works. Consistency in reaching the double-fianchetto shape is more important than the first move.
