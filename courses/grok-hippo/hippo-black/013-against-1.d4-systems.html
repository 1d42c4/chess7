Against 1.d4 Systems

Hippo from Black · Lesson 013 · Direct training

The Hippo is fully universal. Against 1.d4 you can still aim for the same shape.

Typical order: 1.d4 g6 2.c4 Bg7 3.Nc3 d6 4.e4 e6 5.Nf3 Ne7 6.Be2 Nd7 7.0-0 b6 8.Be3 Bb7 etc.

The main difference is that White’s c4-pawn makes ...c5 a different kind of break (often leading to Benoni or Hedgehog-type structures). ...e5 and ...f5 remain thematic.

Against 1.d4 the same principles apply: complete the setup, then choose the break that hurts White’s specific pawn configuration.
