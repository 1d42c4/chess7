Why Knights Belong on d7 and e7

Hippo from Black · Lesson 007 · Direct training

In the Hippo the knights do not go to the classic c6/f6 squares early. Reasons:

  • ...Nd7 supports ...c5 and ...e5 and keeps the c-file and long diagonal open for the b7-bishop.
  • ...Ne7 keeps the f-pawn free for ...f5, supports ...d5 or ...g5 later, and does not block the g7-bishop.

An early ...Nf6 turns the game into a normal Pirc/Modern where White’s theory is much deeper. Stay flexible until the centre is defined.

Only move a knight to f6 or c6 after you have already decided which break you are playing and the position has opened.
