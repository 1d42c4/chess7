Stop thinking in fixed sequences. The Hippo is a final formation: pawns on a6-b6-d6-e6-g6-h6, bishops on b7 and g7, knights on d7 and e7. How you arrive there can vary. The goal is to reach that shape safely, then decide how to strike.

This is why the Hippo frustrates theory-heavy players. They prepare against one move order and you simply take another path to the same position.

Rule: Finish the fence before you fight. Premature breaks while pieces are still undeveloped favour White.

Once the shape is complete you choose the break that punishes White’s specific commitments (...c5, ...d5, ...e5 or ...f5).
