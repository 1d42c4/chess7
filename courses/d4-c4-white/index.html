1.d4 + 2.c4 — White Field Manual

255 short lessons built for someone converting from 1.e4. The Queen's Gambit is the strategic center of the course, but the repertoire also covers what happens when Black refuses to enter it: Indian defenses, Grünfeld, King's Indian, Benoni, Benko, Dutch, Budapest, Old Indian and offbeat first moves. The goal is not to memorize hundreds of branches. The goal is to understand the pawn structures so deeply that theory becomes easier to remember.

Recommended backbone. Against 1...d5, play 2.c4 and learn the Queen's Gambit structures. Against 1...Nf6 2.c4 e6, the initial repertoire uses 3.Nf3 to avoid immediate Nimzo overload while learning QGD/Queen's Indian/Bogo positions; the Nimzo has its own literacy module for later expansion. Against the King's Indian, use a Classical center; against the Grünfeld, learn the Exchange center; against the Dutch, use a c4/g3 framework.
