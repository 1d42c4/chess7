Sources & scope

These lessons are AI-synthesized, original instructional material. They use standard Hippopotamus structure ideas as background; they do not copy text or chess-piece asset files from the sources below.

  • Hippopotamus Defence — Wikipedia: basic structure, historical background, and the standard e5/...d5 and d5/...e5 lock patterns.
  • BetterChess — Hippopotamus Defense: overview of the shell and the ...c5, ...d5, and ...f5 counter-break logic.
  • Lichess study — Hippopotamus Defense: an example annotated move-order treatment.
  • Andrew Martin — The Hippopotamus System of Defence: repertoire topics showing varied move orders and anti-Hippo setups.
  • aileda/chess: the public repository this bundle was prepared to complement.

Important: The Hippo is a practical system, not a guarantee of an objectively equal opening. Concrete calculation and opponent-specific move order matter. The pages deliberately state most strategic rules as “usually,” “when sound,” or “consider” rather than pretending a system move is forced.
